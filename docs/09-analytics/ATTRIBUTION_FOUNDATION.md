# Attribution foundation

Every downstream business outcome should retain the canonical campaign lineage when known: account, person, campaign, enrollment, strategy, offer, message, CTA, sequence, experiment/variant and send IDs.

The first durable event types include meeting_booked and opportunity_created. Attribution is evidence and lineage, not a claim that outreach caused revenue.

The Analytics layer should aggregate these immutable events; Campaign and Mail remain the owners of their operational state.
